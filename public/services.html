Full Spectrum Infrared Sauna

Uses three types of infrared waves for deep tissue healing and detoxification, offering a gentler heat therapy experience than traditional saunas.

Traditional Hot Sauna

Provides dry, ambient heat that promotes detoxification through sweating and reduces inflammation while producing feel-good endorphins.

Cold Plunge

Therapeutic cold exposure that reduces inflammation, activates brown fat, and builds mental resilience.

Contrast Therapy Hot + Cold Alternation

Alternates between hot sauna and cold plunge to enhance recovery, boost circulation, and reset the nervous system.

Halo Red Light Therapy

Combines red light and salt therapy for cellular recovery, skin health, and respiratory benefits.

Normatec Compression Therapy

Uses air compression to massage limbs, improve circulation, and speed up recovery.

Non-invasive electrical stimulation to strengthen pelvic floor muscles and improve core function.

Targeted Cryo Therapy

Precision cold therapy for orthopedic injuries, fat reduction, cryo facials, and skin tightening.
