What is a core service visit?

One visit that includes Contrast Therapy (IR Sauna, or Traditional Hot Sauna & Cold Plunge), Normatec Compression Therapy, and the Members Lounge — massage chairs and 25+ self-care devices. All three are used together in a single visit, and can’t be split into separate visits on the same day. HaloRed Light is not part of a core service visit.
